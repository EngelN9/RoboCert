# RC-002 adjudication prompt v2

In a fresh context, adjudicate two blinded candidate proofs and their four
frozen cross-provider audits against the union ledger. Audit reports are
evidence, not authority. Agreement is not proof; disagreement is not a vote.

1. Determine whether the proofs actually establish the same statement,
   hypotheses, quantifier order, and scope.
2. For every obligation compare their mechanisms as: same, substantially
   equivalent, genuinely independent, only one addresses it, both omit it,
   both address it incorrectly, or conflict.
3. Identify shared nontrivial dependencies `SB-1`... and verify each from first
   principles. Cross-check every forecast point that both proofs pass briefly.
4. Resolve each substantive disagreement `D-1`... from first principles, or
   isolate the exact symbolic/source/human check still required. Never decide by
   majority or style.
5. Record indispensable external results and mark any unresolved locator or
   hypothesis check `SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED`.
6. Produce a reconciliation matrix for every ledger obligation.
7. Give each proof a `V1`...`V5` verdict and one `CV-1`...`CV-7` verdict.
8. State residual shared-blind-spot risk and the strongest proportionate next
   check even if the result is `CV-1`.

Emit one self-contained `ADJUDICATION REPORT` and stop. Do not synthesize or
repair a proof. Treat implementation correspondence as a separate deterministic
evidence gate, not as a conclusion licensed by proof prose.
